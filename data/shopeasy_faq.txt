Refunds: Customers can request a refund within 17 days of delivery. Refunds are paid to the original payment method within 5 business days.
Shipping: Standard shipping takes 4 to 6 business days. Express shipping takes 2 business days and costs 250 rupees extra.
Free delivery: Orders above 1999 rupees get free standard shipping.
Cancellation: An order can be cancelled free of charge until it is packed, usually within 3 hours of placing it.
Returns: Items must be unused and in the original packaging. Returns of opened electronics are not accepted. Electronics means any device that runs on a battery or plugs into a power outlet, for example laptops, phones, tablets and headphones.
Support hours: The support team is available Monday to Saturday, 9 AM to 7 PM IST.
Payment methods: ShopEasy accepts credit cards, debit cards, UPI and cash on delivery for orders up to 5000 rupees.
Loyalty points: Customers earn 2 loyalty points for every 100 rupees spent. 100 points are worth 50 rupees.
Gift cards: ShopEasy gift cards are valid for 12 months from the date of purchase and cannot be refunded.
Warranty: Electronics sold on ShopEasy carry a 1 year seller warranty.
